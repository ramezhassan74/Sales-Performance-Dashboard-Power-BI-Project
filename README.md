# Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI Project

## Overview

Interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales, revenue, customers, products, and territories using a star-schema dataset.

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Business Overview

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Revenue Trend
* Sales Performance Summary

### Sales Insights

* Sales by Territory
* Sales by Shipping Method
* Salesperson Performance
* Category Analysis

### Revenue & Growth

* Monthly Revenue Trend
* MoM Growth Analysis
* Revenue Comparison
* Time Intelligence Metrics

### Orders & Products

* Top Products
* Orders Analysis
* Product Revenue
* Quantity Analysis

### Customer & Territory

* Customer Distribution
* Territory Performance
* Customer Insights
* Regional Analysis
